# November 2, 2026 research update

Historical status at publication. See the [November 3 update](2026-11-03.md)
for current stable R011 and known issues.

T1E2 remains the stable default. R003 remains the experimental Recorder target.
R008 adds source-buildable USB diagnostics; it has not been validated on hardware
and does not resolve the observed R007 USB freeze. Firmware, samples, recordings,
projects, raw captures and generated patch receipts are not published.

## Source added

The R008 recipe includes the R004 buffer-phase accounting, R005 sample
scratch-memory changes and R007 guarded-publication stages. R008 adds 40
rejection/postcheck predicates, per-predicate counters and bounded failure
journals. The R006 timing builder supplies helper definitions but its
instrumentation is not applied to R008.

The guard preserves prior USB audio when either native export fails its buffer
checks. This protects ownership but can leave stale audio indefinitely when all
attempts fail. R008 preserves those decisions so the next capture can identify
the failing condition before changing admission policy.

## Captured behavior

A November 1 bounded capture counted 48 rejected publications among 16,275
attempts. A separate one-minute recording showed 249 common repeated 32-frame
blocks across seven active stereo pairs, repeating audio from 64 frames earlier.
The windows were not sample-aligned: compatible rates do not establish a
one-to-one causal match.

On November 2, USB audio froze while headphones and UI remained functional.
Accepted publications stopped increasing; rejected publications increased by
16,392 over approximately 10.9 seconds. USB queue pointers and transfer states
continued advancing. DSP callbacks and completions also continued, with no
observed increase in codec errors or backlog skips during that capture.

These observations support continuing USB transfers with retained old audio.
They do not identify which buffer condition failed. A fixed buffer-phase
relationship is one hypothesis, not a confirmed cause. No firmware or device
parameter was changed during the fault capture.

## Diagnostic design and boundaries

R008 publishes immutable 28-word snapshots through the existing closed-Recorder
diagnostic phase. Reject records, postcheck records and two counter banks each
have a kind, unique sequence and checksum. Validity masks distinguish current
fields from fields unavailable after an early failure. The host distinguishes
one checksum-complete snapshot from repeated matching cycles.

The 16 existing DMA-register reads retain their addresses and order. There are
no new DMA-register writes. Native audio calculations, rejection sink, codec
processing, Recorder gating, buffer ownership and control-frame ordering remain
unchanged from R007. MAIN/runtime changes only the build marker. Added
observation has bounded instruction paths but can still perturb hardware timing.

Stable START/CURRENT/COUNT observations are not a completed-memory-operation
fence. Register snapshots and elapsed clocks alone cannot prove that a buffer
was stable throughout a copy. Threshold changes or another phase flip would be
speculative without the pending rejection records.

## Validation limits

The private candidate passed exact parent reversal, 64 ABI cases, 160 predicate
routes, two special cases, 16 full native-export comparisons, 64 native-header
comparisons, four complete diagnostic publication windows and four Recorder
gates. Native memory-layout, host-decoder, recovery-loader and container checks
also passed. These are bounded offline checks, not whole-device emulation or a
nested-interrupt execution proof.

Aggregate audit remains **REVIEW**. Encoding novelty, mixed-width SHARC fetch
interpretation and incomplete indirect-store/control-flow coverage remain open.
No failed aggregate component was observed; that is not a whole-image safety
claim. The publication includes source and synthetic tests, not raw receipts or
vendor instructions. Release identities and hardware-validation status are in
[releases/r008.json](../../releases/r008.json).

Next hardware step: install R008 locally, keep the same workload streaming with
Recorder closed, and capture the exact rejecting predicate with screen polling
off. The behavioral correction follows that evidence. Moving USB packing into a
background worker remains an investigation, not an implemented fix.
